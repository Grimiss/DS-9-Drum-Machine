# Classic sample-kit licensing research

Requested machines: Roland TR-606, TR-707 (assuming “7070” meant TR-707), TR-808, TR-909, LinnDrum/Linn drum machines, and Korg KR-55.

Free-to-download or royalty-free-for-music is not automatically a licence to redistribute isolated samples inside a public browser instrument. DS-9's hosted sample files would be downloadable by visitors. Each kit needs explicit instrument/sample-library redistribution permission (or a suitable CC0 licence with credible provenance), not just permission to release songs made with it.

Potential sources to investigate:

- [Samples From Mars](https://samplesfrommars.com/): classic drum-machine libraries and a free-products collection. Verify which machines are currently included free and obtain explicit redistribution permission before bundling; music-production usage terms alone are insufficient.
- [MusicRadar SampleRadar](https://www.musicradar.com/news/sampleradar): free sample collections intended for music production. Verify the individual pack and its terms; do not assume isolated-sample redistribution is permitted.
- [Wavparty](https://wavparty.com/): candidate source for drum-machine recordings. Check the exact pack's licence and provenance; a site-wide assumption is insufficient.

This environment's network proxy denied access to the publisher sites, so current pack availability, coverage of all six machines, prices and licences could not be verified. No third-party samples were downloaded or bundled. There is no confirmed redistributable factory kit yet. The current synthesised voices remain the default; users can import samples locally for their own authorised use.

Before adding any pack, record its source URL, author, original licence text/version, machine mapping, permitted commercial use and redistribution, and required attribution. Avoid claiming official manufacturer affiliation.
